The Game We Love

Under the sun, the players stand,
A bat held tight, a dream in hand.
The bowler runs with fire and pace,
The batter waits, with steady grace.

The ball flies high, the crowd screams loud,
A single shot makes hearts feel proud.
A catch is taken, wickets fall,
Yet hope survives through it all.

From dusty grounds to stadium lights,
Cricket creates unforgettable nights.
Win or lose, we play again,
Through every joy, through every pain.

For every six that touches the sky,
For every tear when dreams pass by,
Cricket is more than bat and ball—
It's the love that brings us all.
